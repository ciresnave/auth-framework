**Breaking: key stores hold symmetric (HMAC and AEAD) keys, and `KeyInfo.public` is optional.** `KeyStore` gains `insert_symmetric` and `export_symmetric`, implemented by `MemoryKeyStore` and `FileKeyStore` (sealed on disk like every other key); an HMAC key signs HS256 inside the store while it is active, shares the key lifecycle (pending, active, retiring, revoked, destroyed, the secret erased on destroy) and is never listed by `publish_jwks`. `rotate` accepts a `LocalKey` or a `SymmetricKey` (`NewKey`) and refuses a replacement of another kind. `KeyInfo.public` is now `Option<PublicJwk>` (`None` for a symmetric key), and a store implementation outside this crate must add the two methods. Evidence: new tests for sign, sealed round trip across a reopen, destroy erasure and kind checks, each shown red by a mutation.
