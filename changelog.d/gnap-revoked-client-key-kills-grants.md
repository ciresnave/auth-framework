**Breaking: a revoked GNAP client key now kills the grants and tokens bound to it (KM5).** `GnapService::revoke_client_key(thumbprint)` drops the key's pending and approved transactions, withdraws its consent records and removes its access tokens, and `revoke_client_key_by_kid(store, kid)` revokes the key in a `KeyStore` first and then does the same, so a compromised client key stops working everywhere at once; a token or consent bound to another key is untouched, and repeating a finished revocation removes nothing and succeeds. Breaking for custom stores: `GnapTokenStore` gains `remove_for_key` and `GnapConsentStore` gains `revoke_for_key`, which an implementation must provide (the in-memory stores do). Tests: revoking removes both of a key's tokens and not a bystander key's, withdraws the consent and drops the pending transaction so it can neither be approved nor continued, and the by-kid form agrees with the key store's own thumbprint.
