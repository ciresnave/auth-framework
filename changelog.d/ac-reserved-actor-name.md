**Breaking: the name `(direct)` is reserved in the access-control engine.** The audit trail records `add_grant` and `remove_grant` under `DIRECT_ACTOR` ("(direct)"), but only `can_edit_rules_about` refused that name, so a principal could be created with it and then used as the actor of `register_new_principal`, `delegate` and `remove_rule`, which recorded its edits as direct calls. `PrincipalRegistry::create` now returns `PrincipalError::InvalidId` for the name, and `add_rule`, `remove_rule`, `delegate` and `register_new_principal` refuse it as the actor (`RuleError::NotAuthorized`, `DelegationError::DelegatorInactive`) without recording anything. A caller that created a principal with that name must rename it. `tests/access_control_reserved_name_tests.rs` fails without the change.
