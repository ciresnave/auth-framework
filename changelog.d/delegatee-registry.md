**Breaking: `delegate` refuses a delegatee that is not an active registered principal.** With a principal registry configured, sharing a grant to an unknown or suspended/retired principal now fails with the new `DelegationError::DelegateeUnknown` or `DelegateeInactive` (both new variants of an exhaustive enum, so a `match` on it must add them). A grant to someone who cannot use it is no longer accepted. Closes G6 (board 384 audit); tests in `access_control_rules_tests`.
