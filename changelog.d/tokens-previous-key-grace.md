**The previous signing key after a rotation is now bounded.** `TokenManager` used to keep verifying tokens under the previous key indefinitely unless an operator called `retire_previous_key`, which nothing did. It now records the rotation time and accepts the previous key only inside a grace window (default 24 hours, `with_previous_key_grace`), only for tokens issued before the rotation and only when their lifetime is at most the configured maximum (default 24 hours, `with_previous_key_max_token_lifetime`); after the window the key retires itself, and the JWKS export drops it. Tokens signed by the current key are unaffected. Refresh tokens signed by the old key therefore stop working after the grace window and holders sign in again. Evidence: eight new `tokens` tests, each shown red by a mutation of the window, issue-time and lifetime checks.
