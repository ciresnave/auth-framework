GNAP consent records (G6 slice 1): when a resource owner approves an interactive transaction and an access token is issued for it, `GnapService` now writes a `GnapConsent` (owner, client key thumbprint, what the access policy granted, time) through the new `GnapConsentStore` trait (`protocols::gnap_consent`; `InMemoryConsentStore` is the default, `GnapService::with_consent_store` swaps it). No token is issued if its record cannot be written. `GnapService::consents_for_owner` lists an owner's records and `revoke_consent` withdraws one and revokes every token issued under it, rotated ones included. Direct grants with no interaction record nothing. Additive; storage for transactions and tokens, and agent/room requirements, are later G6 slices. Eight tests, two mutation checks (drop the token revocation; ignore a store failure) each fail the matching tests.
