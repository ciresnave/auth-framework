**`access_control` delegation with attenuation, chains and cascading revocation; creator grants for new principals (ROADMAP AC2).** `InMemoryEngine::delegate` shares a subset of a grant the delegator holds: the action and object must lie within the source's (`object_within`), the expiry may not outlive the source's, only allow grants can be shared, and the chain is limited to depth 3 (`with_max_delegation_depth`). A delegation is valid only while every grant above it exists and is unexpired, so `remove_grant` also removes everything delegated from it. `register_new_principal` issues the creator grants for just the listed actions on a new object, which is otherwise visible to the creator and the account holder only (`with_account_holder`, not blockable by a deny). `Grant` gains `parent` and `depth` fields.
