Fix: the engine-backed access policy (`EngineGnapPolicy`) no longer copies the `datatypes`, `identifier` and `privileges` of a client's access request into the right it grants. It decides (action, location) pairs only, so a granted right carries none of the three; before, client-requested access-object fields reached the issued token without any policy decision. A deployer policy sees all three fields in the request and decides them itself. Also corrects two rows of `docs/GNAP_RFC9635_GAP.md` (3.4 line reference; 2.4: the user hint's `sub_ids` are added to the subject's, not an intersection). Evidence: `granted_rights_carry_only_what_the_engine_decided` in `access_control/gnap.rs`.
