ROADMAP G1 (additive): `protocols::gnap_wire` holds the RFC 9635 wire types with Appendix E polymorphism (`access_token` object or array, access rights as object or reference string, `client`, `user` and `key` as object or string, the token response with its `manage` object, the registered error codes and both error forms) and the validation the RFC states (flags, labels, bearer with key, the manage rules, public-only JWK, token68). The types of `protocols::gnap` are unchanged; G2 to G5 move the service onto these.
