**Breaking: `GnapTransactionRequest` gains `user: Option<GnapUserRef>` (RFC 9635 2.4).** The service now reads the `user` a client names: its shape is checked (a malformed one is `invalid_request`, nothing stored), and its `sub_ids` join those under `subject` as a hint (11.13), so subject information is released only when the user the AS saw approve matches one of them. A user reference or assertions name nobody the AS can match and change nothing. Migration: struct literals of `GnapTransactionRequest` add `user: None`; or use `GnapTransactionRequest::builder().user(..)`. Evidence: `section_2_4_user_hint_is_checked_and_narrows_subject_release` in `gnap.rs`. **Breaking: `GnapTransaction` gains a public field `subject_updated_at: Option<u64>` (RFC 9635 3.4).** `approve_transaction` records when it learned the subject, and released subject information now carries `updated_at`, an ISO 8601 UTC timestamp of that time (never of the poll); no released `sub_ids`, no `updated_at`. Migration: struct literals of `GnapTransaction` add `subject_updated_at: None`. Evidence: `section_3_4_subject_information_carries_updated_at` in `gnap.rs`. **Breaking: `GnapAccessRequest` gains `datatypes: Vec<String>`, `identifier: Option<String>` and `privileges: Vec<String>` (RFC 9635 8.2).** The service now carries these three fields of an access object from the request to the access policy instead of dropping them, and writes them back out when a request is rebuilt from rights. Migration: struct literals of `GnapAccessRequest` add `..Default::default()` (the struct now derives `Default`); a policy that granted by type, actions and locations alone is unchanged, but may now see a request that asks for a narrower or wider datatype, identifier or privilege and must decide it. Evidence: `requested_rights_carries_datatypes_identifier_privileges` in `gnap.rs`. **Breaking: `GnapTransactionState` gains `Processing` and `Finalized` and is now `#[non_exhaustive]` (RFC 9635 section 1.5).** `begin_processing` moves a pending transaction to `Processing` (it polls like a pending one; a PATCH is refused) and `finalize_transaction` moves a pending or processing one to `Finalized` (its next continuation is refused and removes it). Migration: an exhaustive `match` on `GnapTransactionState` adds a wildcard arm. Evidence: `section_1_5_processing_polls_like_pending_and_finalized_is_refused` in `gnap.rs`.
