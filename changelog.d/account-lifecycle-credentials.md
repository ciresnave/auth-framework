Deactivating or deleting an account, or changing its password, now ends what the account was issued before: its sessions and stored tokens are deleted, and its API keys, access tokens and refresh tokens stop validating, including after the account is reactivated. API-key, session and stored-token validation now also check that the account is live (the access-token and refresh paths already did). A credential issued after the change works. A token minted in the same second as the change is refused, so sign in again.
