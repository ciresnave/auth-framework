ROADMAP G2 (additive): `protocols::gnap_httpsig` verifies GNAP `httpsig` proofs (RFC 9635 section 7.3.1 over RFC 9421 and RFC 9530): the signature base is rebuilt from the request and checked under the client's public JWK (ES256, ES384, Ed25519; PS512 with the `rsa-verify` feature), with the GNAP rules (tag `gnap`, `created` skew, no `alg` parameter, `keyid` equal to `kid`, `@method` and `@target-uri` covered, `content-digest` correct and covered, `authorization` covered for a bound token, nonces not reused). New dependency: `sfv` 0.16 (MIT OR Apache-2.0), approved by the PM; no second crypto stack.
