GNAP continuation route (G7, RFC 9635 section 5): `GnapAsHttp` now routes `POST <grant endpoint>/continue/<transaction id>` (the continue URI the service issues since auth-framework#493), in the axum (`gnap_as_router`) and actix (`gnap_as_configure`) adapters. The continuation token comes in `Authorization: GNAP`, the proof is checked against the configured URL of that transaction, an empty body polls and `{"interact_ref": "..."}` completes an approved interaction; the token rotates on every success. Every refused continuation (unknown transaction, wrong or spent token, wrong or missing interaction reference) is the same 401 `invalid_continuation`, a missing or invalid proof is 401 `invalid_client`, a denied transaction is 403 `user_denied`, other methods are 405. PATCH and DELETE on a grant and polling back-off are not included. No new dependencies.
