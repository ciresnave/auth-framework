**`access_control` 60 s decision cache and live path for sensitive grants (ROADMAP AC3).** `CachedAuthorizer` fronts a fallible `Source` (the in-memory engine implements it): a decision is reused for at most 60 s (`with_ttl` can only shorten it), `Freshness::Live` always asks the source, and an answer is never cached when the action is registered sensitive, the object is on the sensitive list (`mark_object_sensitive`), or any grant that applied is marked sensitive. If the source cannot be reached the decision is `Deny(SourceUnreachable)`: a stale allow is not served after its 60 s, and unreachability never widens access. `invalidate` drops the cache. `Source::generation` (the engine bumps it on every grant, delegation, removal, sensitivity mark and action registration) is checked on every cache hit, so a change, such as a new sensitive deny after an allow was cached, is seen at once rather than after the ttl; a source that does not override it is never served from cache. `InMemoryEngine::registry` is now an accessor, with `registry_mut` for changes. `DenyReason` gains `SourceUnreachable`.
