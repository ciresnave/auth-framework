**`TokenManager::export_jwks` publishes the ES256 and EdDSA keys of a key ring.** It returns the `{"keys":[...]}` JWK Set to serve at `/.well-known/jwks.json`: with a `KeyRing` (`with_keyring`) that is the ring's `Active` and `Retiring` asymmetric keys (EC and OKP JWKs with `kid`, `alg` and `use`, never a private member or an HMAC key), so a token verifies against the published key and a revoked or destroyed key disappears when the ring refreshes (at once if it was revoked through `KeyRing::revoke` or `KeyRing::compromise`); a stale ring refuses to publish instead of advertising a key that may have been revoked. Without a ring it publishes the configured RSA key(s) as JWKs, and an HMAC-only manager an empty set. `export_public_jwks` (RSA only) is unchanged. Evidence: three new `tokens::keyring` tests, each shown red by mutating the staleness check and the ring branch.
