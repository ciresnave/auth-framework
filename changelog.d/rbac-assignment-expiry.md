The RBAC role-assignment endpoints (`POST /rbac/users/{user_id}/roles`, `POST /rbac/bulk/assign`) refuse a request that carries `expires_at` with `EXPIRY_UNSUPPORTED`, and a bulk request with any expiry is refused before any role is granted. Before, the field was accepted and dropped, so the role was granted with no end. Assign without `expires_at` and revoke the role when it should end.
