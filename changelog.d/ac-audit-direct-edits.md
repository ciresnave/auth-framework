**Breaking: the access-control audit trail now records direct grant edits, and says it is diagnostic.** `InMemoryEngine::add_grant` and `remove_grant` changed the grant set without leaving an entry in `audit_log()`; only `add_rule`, `remove_rule` and `delegate` did. They now record `AuditOp::GrantAdded` and `AuditOp::GrantRemoved` under the new `DIRECT_ACTOR` ("(direct)"), since they take no actor; removing an id that does not exist records nothing, and a removal made through `remove_rule` is still recorded once. `AuditOp` gains two variants, so an exhaustive `match` on it must add them. The module docs now state that the trail is an in-memory list with no chaining or signing and is not tamper-evident, so it must not be relied on as evidence. Tests in `tests/access_control_rules_tests.rs` fail without the new records.
