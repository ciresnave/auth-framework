Backup codes are kept only as SHA-256 digests, in the one store the login flow reads (`mfa_backup_codes:{user_id}`); `BackupCodesManager::generate_codes` used to keep them in plaintext under a different key. Codes are now 12 characters from a 32-character alphabet (was 8 digits), compared ignoring case and surrounding spaces. Plaintext codes left by an earlier release are hashed into the store and removed the first time the user's codes are read. Deleting a user also removes the store.
