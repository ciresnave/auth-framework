**Breaking: GNAP polling enforces `wait`, and the client can modify or cancel a pending grant (ROADMAP G5, RFC 9635 sections 5.2-5.4); two fields renamed (SEC-012, #463).** `GnapService` gains `with_poll_wait_secs(n)` (default 5, 0 = off), `modify_transaction_with_proof` (PATCH: a pending transaction only, no `client` or `interact`) and `delete_transaction_with_proof` (DELETE: the client cancels its grant). A poll before `wait` has elapsed is refused with `TOO_FAST`, keeps the continuation token and restarts the clock; `GnapTransaction` gains `last_poll_at`, so literals of it must add the field, and callers that poll faster than 5 seconds must now wait or set `with_poll_wait_secs(0)`. Renamed with their wire names kept: `GnapTransactionRequest.access_token` is now `access_token_request` and `ServiceProviderConfig.change_password` is now `change_password_flag`; `AcmeChallenge.token` carries its `// sec012: public` marker. The HTTP routes for 429 `too_fast`, PATCH and DELETE are the surface lane's. Evidence: 271 lib tests match `gnap` with the CI feature set (6 new), `gnap_conformance` and `gnap_rs_conformance` pass, two tests pin the renamed fields' wire names, and CI clippy and `cargo +stable fmt --check` are clean.
